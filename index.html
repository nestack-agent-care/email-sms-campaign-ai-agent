Email and SMS Campaign AI Agent

The email and SMS campaign AI agent from Nestack Agent Care is built around one idea: refuse the send that the record cannot support. It stages the audience, tests it against suppression and the frequency cap at the hour of release, and leaves the release itself to a named marketer.

Why the send is the last gate

A suppression list is only as good as its last import. A contact who unsubscribed in March can quietly re-enter an uploaded list in June, and an overnight sync turns every synced list into a fresh re-entry risk. Because a send cannot be recalled once it leaves, the agent tests the assembled audience at the moment of release rather than when the list was built.

What the agent does

  • Assembles the audience and marks any segment built from inferred attributes as inferred.
  • Applies one frequency cap across both email and text, not separate budgets per channel.
  • Drafts the send, including a footer with a working opt-out and a physical postal address.
  • Holds the staged send, with its audience, suppression state and caps, for a named marketer to release.

Failure modes Nestack evaluates

Nestack places seven failure modes at the stage where each first shows: a stale audience read, suppression assumed rather than tested, a cap read from one channel, a thin audience passed forward, a suppression entry bound to the wrong list, a release with no audience recorded, and a silent suppression regression after a platform change. Correction rates are reported by list source, since uploaded and synced lists tend to carry most of the rework.

Build scope

A typical build runs twelve workstreams across six weeks: discovery, sources, audience assembly, evaluation, integration, then production validation and handover. Consent capture and revocation intake belong to a sibling agent, so this one assembles no consent evidence and only refuses to send what the record cannot support.
